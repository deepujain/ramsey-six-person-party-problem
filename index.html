A party with a little secret.

Imagine you invite people to a party. For every pair, they either know each other or have never met.

We’re looking for a group of three where either:

  • All three know each other, or
  • None of the three know each other.

How many people must attend to guarantee one of those groups, whatever their relationships?

Tap three guests to see their relationships. Gold ribbons mean they know each other. Dashed blue ribbons mean they have never met.

The answer is six: the classical Ramsey theorem R(3,3) = 6. Read the complete proof.
